    READ THE BLADE, NOT THE BODY

    PARRY IS
    THE DOOR.

    A parry turns a killing blow into a broken stance. The window is brief, the reward is total: no damage, a stagger, mana, and enough time to answer with steel or spell.

    HOLD S TO GUARD. GUARD SHRUGS OFF CHIP DAMAGE — BREAKERS IGNORE IT.

    01 / BLADE

    STRIKE & BREAK

    • J / ZLight strike. Press again in recovery to chain three hits.
    • K / XHeavy breaker. Slow, huge damage, breaks guard.
    • S / ↓Hold guard. Chip damage is reduced, but breakers punish it.

    02 / ARCANE

    SPELLS

    • 1EMBER LANCE — a fast burning projectile. Can be parried back.
    • 2FROST NOVA — a close frost ring that chills and slows.
    • 3STORM SIGIL — delayed lightning at a marked spot. Move.

    Frost unlocks after gate 2. Storm after gate 4. Mana regenerates; parries refund it.

    03 / MOTION

    FOOTWORK

    • ADMove. You face your opponent automatically.
    • SPACEJump. Aerials are legal.
    • SHIFTDash. Brief invulnerability, useful against sigils and lances.

    04 / ASCENT

    PROGRESS

    • SPWin duels to earn skill points. They persist forever.
    • 10Gates, two crowned bosses, then an endless ascension.
    • ★Defeat is not the end. Retry the gate with your ranks intact.

    Higher difficulty pays more SP. Touch controls appear automatically on phones and tablets.

    Tip: bait a heavy, parry the wind-up, then cast through the stagger.
